Privacy & Data Use

Your data lives in your own private folder. Email is used for verification, password reset, and optional reminders. SMS reminders (Twilio) are opt-in. Photos save privately with your entries. Nothing is ever sold or shared. Your User ID arrives in your welcome email — keep it for support.

Privacy Policy — Full Language

Data Protection: Your personal information (name, email, date of birth, religious preferences, fasting choices, daily entries, and photos) is stored securely in a private Google Drive folder managed by Family Covenant Mundo. Your data is accessible only to you and the app administrator for support purposes.

Email Usage: Your email address is used solely for account verification, password resets, and (if you opt in) daily covenant reminders. We do not send marketing emails, and we never sell or share your email with third parties.

SMS Reminders (Opt-In): If you provide a phone number, SMS reminders may be sent via Twilio, our SMS service provider. You may opt out at any time by removing your phone number from your profile. Standard message rates may apply.

Photo Storage: Photos you upload with entries are stored as files in your private folder and linked from your log. Photos are never shared publicly.

Data Retention: Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days.

Third Parties: We use Google Drive (storage) and Twilio (SMS only). No other third parties receive your data.

Terms of Service — Full Language

Appropriate Use: This service is for personal spiritual growth and community covenant participation. Use for harassment, abuse, impersonation, or unlawful activity will result in account suspension.

Minors: Users under 18 should participate with parent/guardian awareness. Age group information is collected to provide age-appropriate experiences.

Service Availability: Family Covenant Mundo is provided "as-is" without warranties of any kind. We take reasonable measures to protect your information but are not liable for data loss due to technical failures.

Support: Your unique User ID (provided at registration) helps us assist you. Include it in support requests.

Changes: We may update these terms; material changes will be communicated via email.
